- **hande: a bot that stops reading its socket is dropped instead of left half-connected (#2740).** When an outbound frame could not be flushed within 5 s, the transport marked the client closed but kept the socket and its read loop. The bot never saw `worker_gone`: the turn in flight ended without a `result`, every later turn it sent on that connection ran and its answer was discarded, `health` reported no bot, and a second connection was accepted next to it — whose turn was then cancelled as `worker_gone` when the first socket finally closed. The transport now aborts the connection on that timeout (the bot restarts and reconnects, as after a bridge restart), and a connection's teardown only abandons a turn that connection sent.
