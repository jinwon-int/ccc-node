- **feat(bridge/hande): a long final answer goes out as page-numbered bubbles (#2856).** The native bot bounds every message it posts at 8 KiB and cut the rest with `…(길어서 줄임)`, so the end of a long briefing never reached the owner. The hande transport now splits a `complete` answer above that bound (`core/hande/reply_split.py`): the first N-1 parts go out as `interim` frames and the last as the `result`, in order, each starting with `(k/N)`. An answer that fits one message is sent exactly as before, unmarked. Cuts prefer a heading, then a blank line, then a list item; a cut inside a code fence closes and reopens it, a cut inside a table repeats the header rows, and the last `[답변 필요]` block stays whole in the last part. No bot change is needed (today's bot posts any non-status interim as a message); part frames carry `kind: "part"`, `part` and `parts` for a bot that later wants to treat them differently. New hande config knobs: `reply_split` (default **on**; needs `interim_frames`), `bot_reply_max_bytes` (8192) and `bot_interim_max` (40), the last two mirroring the bot's own bounds. Because the bot drops interims past its per-turn cap silently, the bridge counts the interims a turn used and leaves the answer unsplit when the pages would not fit (`reply_split_skipped`). Pages stop at the first one the bot does not take (`reply_split_failed`), and with `notice_frames` on the room is told how many arrived. The conversation record keeps the whole answer as one entry. `health_signals()` adds `reply_split`, `replies_split`, `reply_split_skipped` and `reply_split_failed`.
