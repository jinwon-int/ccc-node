- **Detached systemd jobs are external waits now (#2605 layer 3).** `external_wait_cli register --source detached-job --unit <job> --log <path> --summary …` watches a `bridge-safe-detached-run` transient unit: the monitor polls the log's `EXIT=<n>` marker locally (8 KiB tail read + `systemctl is-active`, no GitHub) with a 60 s backoff ceiling, shows `⏳ Running detached · detached job <unit> — …` on the route's status message, and wakes the conversation like a CI wait when the job ends — `success` for `EXIT=0`, `failure` otherwise (`exit=<n>` in the event and `Exit code: n` in the notice), `lost` when the unit is gone without a marker (no log, or a log quiet for 15 min). The resume prompt names the unit and the log path, never its content. `claude/hooks/lib/detached_jobs.py register` arms this automatically on bridge nodes inside a turn (`--no-wait` / `CCC_DETACHED_JOBS_NO_WAIT=1` opt out; `--registry` never arms), so the recipe's job registry and the bridge's ⏳ line come from one command. This is the one background path that survives a bridge restart.
