- **Matrix frontend: fleet-relay mode can keep owner-personal notices in the agent's own room (#2227).** Relay mode (#2182) sent *every* push-spool record to the 🔔 플릿 알림 room, so on a Termux node the important-mail notices (`event: mail`, 24 records), the daily schedule brief and lotto results moved out of the owner's direct room along with the operational alerts. New `CCC_PUSH_FLEET_RELAY_DIRECT_EVENTS` (comma-separated `event` values, default empty = unchanged) routes the listed events to the owner room as the agent — same format, dedup window and rate limit — while everything else still goes to the relay. The early relay notifier (#2207) now receives the opened transport; before that, and on a transient room failure, a listed record waits in the spool (later listed records wait behind it) without blocking relay records, and the transport-failed exit drain counts only relay-bound records. No owner room configured → the listed record goes to the relay rather than nowhere. Relay-mode `run()` no longer looks up the owner room at start, so a failed lookup can no longer take the relay drain down.
