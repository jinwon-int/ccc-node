- **hande (#2590 A): `wait_status_frames` shows registered external waits after the turn ends.** Matrix keeps one edited/moved/redacted status message per route (#2088); the hande port has no edit or delete, so `HandeWaitStatusMixin` re-sends the shared renderer's `⏳ Waiting for results · …` text as a `notice` frame with `kind:"status"` — posted by a status-v1 bot as a transient `status-<uuid>` line (bottom of the room and room-list preview in the apps, never history or push). Re-sent on `turn_closed`, on a text change and every `wait_status_interval_s` (default 120, 30..240, under the apps' 5-minute expiry) while a wait is monitored; nothing while a turn runs in that room, while no bot is connected, or once nothing is monitored. Default off; needs `notice_frames` too. Enable only after the node's bot knows `notice` + `kind:"status"`. `health_signals()` reports `wait_status_frames` and `wait_status_sent`.
