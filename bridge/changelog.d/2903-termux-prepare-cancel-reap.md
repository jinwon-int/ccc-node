- **termux-prepare: a cancelled stage no longer hangs while reaping its child (#2903).** A SIGINT/SIGTERM that interrupts the timed `Popen.wait` can raise between the wait's internal lock acquire and the `try` that releases it (reproduced on CPython 3.11, 3.12 and 3.14). The lock stayed held, so the cleanup `child.wait()` after `killpg` blocked forever and the cancelled run never wrote its receipt. `Runner.run` now reaps through `os.waitpid` directly, so it does not depend on that lock. The flaky `test_cancellation_kills_workers_after_group_leader_exits` replaces its fixed sleeps with readiness polling: the driver is stopped (`T`), then the leader is released and exits unreaped (`Z`), then the cancel signal is sent. A deterministic regression test pins the leaked-lock window.
