- **hande (#2602): with `status_frames`, the last `⏳` line is re-posted right after every interim.** status-v1 apps drop the sender's transient status bubble when a normal message from it lands, so on turns that post progress interims the `⏳ Working` line vanished until the next heartbeat (5 s at best). A read-only relay count over 24 h showed the chattiest nodes keeping the line on screen only 67–75 % of turn time (others 86–98 %). `_HandeSink.interim()` now re-sends the turn's last `⏳` text as a `kind:"status"` interim immediately after the interim frame; nothing before the first heartbeat, nothing in `status_interims` mode (a plain-interim status would add room messages), nothing when `interim_frames` is off (no interim went out). `health_signals()` reports `status_resent`.
