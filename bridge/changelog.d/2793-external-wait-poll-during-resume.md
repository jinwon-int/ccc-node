- **fix(external-wait): keep polling while a Telegram resume turn runs (#2793).** The monitor polled waits and delivered wakes in one loop, and on Telegram a wake awaits a whole agent turn, so for minutes no other PR-checks, merge-queue, Actions-run or detached-job wait was polled. Polling and wake delivery are now two loops in `ExternalWaitMonitor.run`. Wakes are still delivered one at a time. Same-chat ordering, the `resuming` journal (#2787), the daily resume cap and retry backoff are unchanged, and the poll loop nudges the wake loop so a wake it journals is not held for a 5 s tick. Each loop keeps its own idle fast-path identity. An exception in one loop is logged and the other keeps running. Cancelling `run()` cancels and awaits both loops. Matrix and Hande resumers return immediately and see no behaviour change. Regression tests use a blocking resumer; they fail against the old single loop.
