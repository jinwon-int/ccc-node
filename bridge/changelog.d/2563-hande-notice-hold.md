- The 「한데」 bridge no longer loses notices raised while the native bot is reconnecting (#2563). The startup banner is posted ~2 s after a restart while the bot reconnects to the responder socket ~10 s later, so every restart logged `hande notice dropped`; a background-task completion or CI-wait result in that gap was lost the same way. With `notice_frames` on, such notices (and self-job output whose send fails) are now held in order and flushed when the bot connects, before waiting self-jobs run — at most 16, oldest dropped first, aged out after 10 minutes. `health_signals` gains `notices_pending`; the drop log line now names its reason (`frame-off`, `held-overflow`, `held-expired`).
