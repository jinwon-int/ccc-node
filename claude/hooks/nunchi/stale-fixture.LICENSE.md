# stale-fixture.json — attribution

`stale-fixture.json` is a 40-scenario subset (20 T1 co-referential + 20 T2 propagated implicit
conflicts, sampled with seed 20261022) of **STALE: State Tracking And Latent Evaluation**
(Chao, Bai, Sheng, Li, Sun — arXiv:2605.06527), distributed at
`https://huggingface.co/datasets/STALEproj/STALE` under **CC BY 4.0**
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Only `uid`, `type`, `M_old`, `M_new`,
`dim1_query` and `explanation` are kept; the long-context haystack sessions (which embed
LongMemEval-derived material) and the dim2/dim3 queries are not included. No changes were
made to the retained text.
