- **wiki-record / wiki-log: insert new operating-log entries on a line-anchored match, never a first-substring match.** The `pages/log.md` digest quotes the heading pattern mid-line (`` `## [LOG-YYYYMMDD-<node>-<seq>]` ``), so a node that located "the newest heading" with `str.index('## [LOG-')` spliced its entry into the digest twice on 2026-11-05 (#2671; seoyoon-family-wiki #9254, then again after the #9273 repair; fixed by #9273/#9276). The skill (Claude + Codex) and the `/wiki-log` command now give a `re.search(r"^## \[LOG-", text, re.M)` insertion snippet, warn against substring matches, and require the log diff to be additions only with the digest lines unchanged before `wiki-agent pr`.
