- **Nunchi can age out stale `task-progress` facts, opt-in and off by default (#2759).** On 2026-11-11, 2,789 of the 5,078 open `task-progress` facts fleet-wide were older than 30 days: G1 closes an in-flight fact only when a matching completion fact arrives, and only `observation` had an age gate. `NUNCHI_TASK_PROGRESS_TTL_DAYS` adds one for `task-progress`, run in the same opportunistic `snapshot`/`assemble` step as the observation sweep (skipped with one stderr line when the write lock is busy). Unset, `0`, negative, non-finite and unparsable values all mean no sweep, so nothing changes on a node until its owner sets the variable. The sweep never closes a user-stated fact (`source_rank` 3), a review-flagged fact or another kind. A close sets `valid_to` only and adds a body-free row to the new `ttl_close` ledger (created only by an enabled sweep); a fact is closed by age at most once, so one the owner reopens stays open. `nunchi.py metrics` now prints `task_progress_open`, `task_progress_open_over_30d` (also with the TTL off), `task_progress_ttl_days` and `task_progress_ttl_closed`. `docs/nunchi-curation-authority.md` lists the pass under Tier 1 and `curation-authority.test.sh` pins its default and exclusions.
