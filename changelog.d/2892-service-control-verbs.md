- **`ccc-service-control` takes `start`, `stop` and `status` with per-unit verbs (#2892 B1).** The root-installed wrapper now accepts `<restart|start|stop|status> <unit.service>`. Each line of `/etc/ccc-node/service-control.allow` may name the verbs a unit can receive (`example-inbox.service start,stop,status`); a bare unit line keeps its old meaning (`restart` plus read-only `status`), so existing allowlists behave as before. A unit listed twice or an unknown verb fails closed, and `enable`/`disable`/`mask`/`daemon-reload` are never passed. `status` runs a fixed `systemctl show --property=…` and prints no journal lines. Start/stop are registered as the side-effect operation `service_control.lifecycle`. Installing or widening the wrapper on a node is still a separate operator step (`docs/service-control.md`).
