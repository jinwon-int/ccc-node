- **SessionEnd skill drafting and the nightly autosave sweep now share one regrowth ledger, so a transcript is drafted once instead of twice (#2642, #2239 follow-up b).** The 2026-11-03 six-node measurement found 17 of 68 weekly pending drafts (25%; 17/79 the week before) were the same session drafted by both the `sessionend` hook and the sweep's `manual` pass — the hook never read or wrote `skill-autosave.seen`, so the sweep saw an unprocessed transcript, and a session the sweep had drafted mid-flight was drafted again when it ended. `skill-review.sh` now records `<session>\t<ts>\t<size>` in that ledger after a hook-triggered review finishes (any staged count; not after an extraction failure), which the sweep's existing regrowth check already honours, and skips a hook-triggered review when the ledger holds the session at less than `CCC_SKILL_AUTOSAVE_REGROWTH_BYTES` (16 KiB) growth, logging `skip reason=already-drafted`. The `manual` trigger is exempt (the sweep keeps its own bookkeeping; an operator's manual call always runs), the check runs after the cooldown so existing cooldown behaviour is unchanged, and `CCC_SKILL_REVIEW_LEDGER_DEDUPE=off` restores the independent paths. Expected effect on pending inflow: about −25% with no quality loss. Tests cover both directions, the exemption, the opt-out, and the sweep honouring a hook-written row.
