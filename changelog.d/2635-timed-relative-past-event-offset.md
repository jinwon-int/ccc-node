- **Relative-mode past-tense incident offsets are demoted to low confidence as `past-event-offset` (#2635).** Incident forensics such as family-messenger#616 "보충이 15시간 뒤에야 일어났고" said how long something already took, not when a test ends, yet reached the owner notice as high. A relative term now gets the new reason when a past-tense predicate (`일어났고`, `걸렸다`, `였다`, `이었습니다`, `했습니다`, …) follows it within 25 characters in the same clause. Like the other wording rules it never applies when the paragraph names an observation, and every unquoted term must be explained. Future windows such as "배포 48시간 뒤 재확인", "24시간 뒤 판정하겠습니다", "24시간 뒤 에러가 없었다면 승격" and "48시간 뒤 정상화됐는지 확인" stay high.
- **Relative-mode clock glosses and generic distributive durations are demoted to low confidence (#2635).** `anchored-clock-gloss`: a term that only glosses a month-day clock time next to it (family-messenger#616 "다음 보충은 **10-31 11:27:19**(약 15시간 뒤)") is low; `10-31 11:27(15시간 뒤 재확인)` and an offset with no adjacent clock stay high. `_dates_in` is unchanged. `generic-duration`: a `씩` term in a "can/could" clause (ccc-node#2659 "장애가 며칠씩 조용히 이어질 수 있습니다") is low; "며칠 뒤 판정" and "며칠씩 관측할 수 있습니다" stay high.
