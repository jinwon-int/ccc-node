- **The timed-test relative scan now has its own cron installer and runs next to the expired scan (#2544).** `scripts/install-timed-relative-scan-cron.sh` (dry-run by default, `--apply`, `--remove`) renders one managed entry, marker `# ccc-node:timed-relative-scan`, that runs `timed_test_deadline_scan.py --mode relative --notify LEVEL --exit-nonzero-on-findings` once a day at 09:25 KST against the deadline scan's repos file, logging to `timed-relative-scan.cron.log`, with its own install record and `CCC_TIMED_RELATIVE_SCAN_*` env overrides. `install-timed-test-deadline-scan-cron.sh --mode relative` used to replace the `expired` entry, so no node ran relative mode; it now delegates to the new installer and leaves the expired entry and record alone. ccc-doctor registers the marker (`cron gen timed-relative-scan`), and relative-mode notices point to the new log. The deadline installer's gen stamp changes, so self-update re-applies its recorded argv once.
- **Relative-mode false positives are demoted to low confidence with a reason code (#2544).** The 2026-10-27 audit found 7 false positives in 10 relative findings. `external-author` (non-fleet login), `quoted-term` (term in quotes, inline code or a fenced block), `checkbox-verdict-recorded` (task-list item with a later verdict comment), `operational-wording` ("당분간"), `planning-estimate` ("스파이크(1~2주)") and `process-duration` ("수일 소요", "심사(통상 수일)") stay in the log but leave the high-only owner notice. The wording rules never apply to a paragraph that names an observation (관찰, 관측, 소크, 카나리, soak, canary, …), so a2a-nexus#2395 and family-messenger#404 stay high. One undemoted paragraph keeps an issue high. `gh issue list` now also requests `author`. Expired and followup output is unchanged.
- **Docs: an automated recheck of a timed test must post its verdict, or a verdict draft, to the issue (#2544).** It must also cover every criterion in the booking. The contract is in `docs/timed-test-deadline-scan.md`.
