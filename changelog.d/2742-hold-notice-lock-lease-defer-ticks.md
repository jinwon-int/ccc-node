- **self-update: a hold notice no longer swallows the failure that follows it, a long prepare keeps its lock, and the busy-deferral cap protects more than one tick (#2742, third group).** (1) When the external restart command hands a busy frontend to a detached follower, the owner gets a hold notice ("실패가 아니라 보류"). It wrote the same once-per-target marker as a failure notice, so a real failure for that target on a later tick — the follower gave up, the old generation still serves — was dropped as `already-notified` and the owner's last word stayed "보류". The marker now records what the owner was told (`hold`/`failure` on line 2; a one-line marker counts as `failure`): a hold suppresses only another hold. A later tick is still a hold while a follower is inside its own wait (ledger `pending` without `done`, epoch + `max_wait` + 300 s not passed) and a failure once every follower is done or overdue, so a follower that died without writing `done` cannot keep the notice suppressed. One hold and one failure notice per target at most; #2148's one-alarm rule is unchanged. (2) The lock directory is stale 30 minutes after its mtime, a `prepare-cmd` may run for two hours and nothing refreshed the mtime, so the next tick could take the lock of a live run. The holder now leases the lock: mtime = now + the step's budget before the prepare command and the external restart command (plus its health wait; capped at 7200 s), back to now afterwards and at stage boundaries. A run that dies mid-step is recovered 30 minutes after its lease ends. (3) `CCC_SELF_UPDATE_MAX_DEFER_SECONDS` (3600) is shorter than the gap between ticks, so the cap meant "defer once": the second busy tick always proceeded and killed whatever turn was running. The cap now also needs `CCC_SELF_UPDATE_MIN_DEFER_TICKS` deferred ticks (default 2, 0..20, `0` = seconds only): the third consecutive busy tick proceeds. The pre-restart recheck (#2667) applies the same floor per target generation. A busy node can therefore wait one tick longer for an update than before. `ccc-self-update.test.sh` 462→488 PASS; 21 of the new assertions fail on the previous script.
