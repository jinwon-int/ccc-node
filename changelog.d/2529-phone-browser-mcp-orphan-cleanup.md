- **`phone-browser-mcp` no longer leaks SSH tunnels and phone `adb forward`s when the wrapper is SIGKILLed (#2529).** Each session now records its stem `$STATE_DIR/<host>.<wrapper-pid>.<local-port>` as `.ctl` (ControlMaster socket) plus `.state` (host, adb serial, phone forward port — written right after `adb forward tcp:0`). At startup, before opening its own tunnel, the launcher sweeps its own host's stems (names in that scheme only, files owned by this uid, never symlinks): a stem whose wrapper PID is gone, or alive but not a `phone-browser-mcp.sh` wrapper (PID-reuse guard via `/proc/<pid>/cmdline`, `ps` fallback), gets `ssh -O exit` (bounded), its socket removed and exactly the recorded forward removed via the phone's adb; sockets without a `.state` (pre-#2529) only get their tunnel closed and phone forwards are left alone. `.state` files are claimed by atomic rename, and kept for a retry when the phone is unreachable. The sweep is best-effort, time-bounded (`PHONE_BROWSER_SWEEP_BUDGET`, default 20 s, `0` disables) and logs to stderr only. A lightweight reaper (the script re-run as `--reap-after`, under `setsid` when available, stdin/stdout on `/dev/null`, log `$STATE_DIR/reaper.log`) polls the wrapper every `PHONE_BROWSER_WATCH_INTERVAL` seconds (default 3, `0` disables) and reclaims the session if the wrapper dies without running its traps; normal cleanup stops it. Normal cleanup itself is now time-bounded and keeps `.state` for the next launch when the phone is unreachable.
