- The live MemPalace `repair-status` read in `ccc_memory_probe.py` (`ccc-memory-check.sh`, doctor sweeps) is now bounded by `CCC_NUNCHI_MEMPALACE_INDEX_PROBE_TIMEOUT_SEC` (1-60 s, default 15) instead of a fixed 5 s (#2870). A healthy 3.4 GB palace whose reads take 5-6.4 s was reported as `index-timeout`, degrading mempalace and raising a fleet-doctor `DRIFT`. Unset, non-integer or out-of-range values use the default; the refresh-side `CCC_NUNCHI_MEMPALACE_REPAIR_STATUS_TIMEOUT_SEC` is unchanged.
