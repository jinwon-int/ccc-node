- **A fleet-identity hit no longer turns a whole promote batch into a human draft (#2435 S4, with the arbitration switch at `true`).** `promote` un-stages an approved candidate whose `SKILL.md` names a fleet node or product and records an `a2a-identity-hold`; the rest of the batch is staged without it, the held candidate takes no batch slot, and an open batch that still carries hits is superseded by the clean one. The new `_sweep_identity_holds` dispatches one `identity` revise round per lineage (one blocking finding per hit line, outside the round limit and the final round, not an arbitration trigger); a republished tree returns as a fresh intake PR that is re-reviewed and re-scanned, and anything else — a failed round, a hit surviving the lineage's round, or nothing dispatchable for 48 h — closes the intake PR as `archived:identity-unresolved` (an owner reopen is left alone). Field case 2026-10-21: promote batches fleet-skills #548/#551/#552 (7-8 approved candidates each) held as `needs-human-identity` by three `hermes` lines in one candidate.
