- **fleet-alerts 「한데」 lane: notice acknowledgements (family-messenger#703).** `fleet_alerts_sender_hande.py` now sends each `notice` frame with an `"id"` (the row's txn, or `hande-<row id>`) and keeps the mirror row until the relay bot answers on the same connection: `notice_ack` deletes it and records a strong `hande-ack` receipt (ref = relay seq), so a 「한데」-only delivery now counts as `notified`; `notice_nack` `rate` retries after ~60 s, `relay`/`conflict`/`client_id_reuse` resend on the next round, any other reason is logged (reason only, never the body) and the row is dropped with a `hande-dropped` receipt.
