- **Nunchi curation authority tiers are now declared and enforced (#2464 제안 4).** `docs/nunchi-curation-authority.md` lists every path that writes `peer_facts` by who triggers it. Tier 1 is unattended (ingest, the judge-batch cron, snapshot): it may only add facts, close them under a deterministic gate (G1, B2, observation TTL) and set or clear review flags. Tier 2 is the owner-invoked bulk passes (`review-stale`, `constraints-triage`, `conflict-sweep`), dry-run by default. Tier 3 is the owner per-fact commands. No path deletes a fact row. `nunchi.py review` now ends with a one-line authority statement, the usage text gains the authority block plus the missing `review` and `retag` entries, and the new `curation-authority.test.sh` fails if any nunchi source deletes from `peer_facts`, if judge-batch writes anything but `review=0`, or if `conflict-sweep` gains a `valid_to` write.
