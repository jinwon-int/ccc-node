- **New `scripts/remote-heartbeat-watch.sh`: page from another node when a scheduled job stops refreshing its heartbeat file (a2a-nexus#2428 follow-up).** The external GitHub PR/Issue watch sat dead for three months after its node's scheduler stopped on 2026-07-13 — a silent watch looks exactly like a quiet one. The script measures the file's age on the target, pages `NEW` / `STILL` (every `--remind-hours`, default 6) / `RECOVERED`, leaves an unreachable target to the bridge watch, and rejects shell metacharacters in `--path`. Recipe in `docs/fleet-watch.md`.
