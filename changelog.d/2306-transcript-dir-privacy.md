- **fix(memory): an umask-created Claude transcripts directory no longer silently drops every distill (#2306).** The distill snapshot refused a group/other-accessible `~/.claude/projects/<slug>` (0755/0775 from the umask) and every job terminalized as `invalid_snapshot_request` with no log line — two nodes lost days of memory. The bridge now tightens its own transcripts directory to 0700 at start (creating it 0700 if absent) and on read, through an `O_NOFOLLOW` descriptor, logging a warning; symlinks, non-directories and foreign owners are still refused, and every transcript file keeps its own uid/nlink/mode check. A terminal snapshot refusal now logs its reason (plain reader messages only; `ValueError` subclasses log just their class name). `ccc-doctor` gains `claude transcripts dir` (served directory mode) and `distill snapshot refusals` (7-day `invalid_snapshot_request` count), both read-only.
