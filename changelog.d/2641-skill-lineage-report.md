- **New read-only `scripts/ccc-skill-lineage-report.py` regroups the skill-promotion ledger by intake lineage (#2641, #2239 follow-up a).** The #2239 second verdict (2026-11-02) found that 48 of 56 weekly `revise` verdicts were first reviews and 35 of 41 `approve`s were republished PRs: the pipeline converges as revise → `a2a-revise-result` → republish → approve, so the verdict-level revise share counts rounds, not outcomes, and the "<40%" target was unreachable by design. The report joins `a2a-verdict` rows (via the `skills_intake_review-pr<N>-<node>-<stamp>` task id) with the `a2a-intake-superseded` and republished `a2a-revise-result` links into lineages, cohorts them by first consumed verdict in `[--since, --until)`, and prints the replacement metrics — final approve rate (decided / all), mean rounds, first-review approve rate, outcome split (approved / rejected / dropped / in_progress), per author node — next to the old `legacy_revise_ratio` on the same window so the two can be compared. `malformed` / `infra_failed` verdicts are excluded from rounds and listed separately, as the #2239 comments did. `--json` includes every lineage. Stdlib only; never writes.
