- skills: `scheduled-verification-with-session-fallback` no longer presents a `systemd-run` transient timer as the default for every wait. A transient unit lives under `/run/systemd/transient/` and is gone after a reboot, so it is now tier 1 only for short waits that cannot cross one; any multi-hour or overnight wait, and any observation-end or hold-expiry action, goes to a persistent unit (`Persistent=true`) or crontab. The skill also adds a pre-check for a reboot that is already queued (`/run/systemd/shutdown/scheduled`, `reboot-required`, the `Automatic-Reboot` policy) and notes that `Automatic-Reboot "false"` does not cancel a queued `shutdown -r`. Field case: a 24 h production observation lost its T+24h snapshot and its hold-expiry timer to an unattended 03:15 reboot (#2287).
