- **Nunchi can retire the constraints of finished sessions, opt-in and off by default (#2838).** On 2026-11-20 on one node, 629 of 810 open constraints were stored under a `session:*` peer and 625 of them came from sessions idle for 7+ days; because the snapshot fills its constraint block newest-first, they held 20 of its 49 constraint lines. `NUNCHI_SESSION_CONSTRAINT_TTL_DAYS` closes an open `session:*` constraint once its session has produced no fact of any kind for that many days. It runs in the same opportunistic `snapshot`/`assemble` step as the other age sweeps, parses its value the same way (unset, `0`, negative, non-finite and unparsable all mean no sweep) and never closes a node or person constraint, a user-stated (`source_rank` 3) or review-flagged fact, or another kind. A close sets `valid_to` only and adds a body-free `ttl_close` row with `kind=constraint`; a reopened fact stays open. `nunchi.py metrics` now prints `session_constraint_open`, `session_constraint_open_idle_over_7d`, `session_constraint_ttl_days` and `session_constraint_ttl_closed`. `docs/nunchi-curation-authority.md` lists the pass under Tier 1 as the only unattended path that closes a constraint, and `curation-authority.test.sh` pins its default and exclusions.
