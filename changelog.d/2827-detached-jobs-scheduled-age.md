- **Detached jobs (#2827): a scheduled job's week in the sweep now starts at its due time, and `schedule` warns that a reboot erases its timer.** The SessionStart sweep dropped every unacked record seven days after `started_at`, so a job armed more than a week ahead left the list before it fired; when a reboot then took its transient timer, nothing reported it `lost` (observed 2026-11-17: a reminder registered 11-13 for 12-02 would have left the sweep on 11-20). `schedule` now records `scheduled_epoch` for a one-shot `--at` instant (`YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM[:SS]` with an optional zone name or `UTC`), and the age cap counts from that time; a row written before this change takes the same due time from its `scheduled_at`. A recurring or relative spec (`05:00`, `Mon *-*-* 05:00`) has no single due time and keeps the registration-time week. The `schedule` docstring, usage text and the `bridge-safe-detached-run` skill now say the timer is transient and lost on reboot, that waits spanning nights or days need a persistent `Persistent=true` unit, and to save and re-create transient timers around a reboot (same property as #2287). Unchanged: classification of done/running/lost, the 7-day length, records without `scheduled_at`.
