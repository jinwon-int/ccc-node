- **Undecided autosave drafts are pre-screened by the fleet intake reviewer before a human sees them (#2183).** `ccc-skill-autosave.sh run` step 2c3 (`claude/hooks/skill-review/prescreen.py`) builds the same `skills.skill-intake-review.v1` packet the publisher dispatches and runs `skills-intake-review-handler.sh` locally (rubric 2026-08-28.2, no broker, no edge secret) over up to `CCC_SKILL_PRESCREEN_MAX_PER_RUN` drafts per run (default 20, `0` = off), oldest first. `reject` with a blocker finding — or a name that collides with an installed skill — moves the draft into `skill-autosave-archive/prescreen-reject-<date>/` with a `manifest.jsonl` row (`pending-restore <name>` brings it back); every other verdict stays in the queue as `prescreen.json`, which `/skillsuggest` now shows first. A reviewer failure records `status: error` and moves nothing; three consecutive failures stop the run. `ccc-skill-autosave.sh prescreen [--dry-run]`, `status` and a `ccc_doctor` "skill-autosave prescreen" row (reviewer down / stale / all-error) make the step observable.
