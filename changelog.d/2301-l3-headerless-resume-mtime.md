- **fix(memory): a replayed distill backlog no longer overwrites a newer CLI resume (#2301 L3).** In node-global (off/curated) mode the bridge sink orders `resume.md` by the bridge-recorded `captured_at` in its header, but a resume with no stamped capture — the hook-path CLI resume (header-less) or a pre-#2297 audience header — was always overwritten, so a month-old backlog could replace today's CLI resume. Such a resume is now kept unless the new capture is strictly later than its file mtime (both host-clock times, never model output). A job without a capture time still overwrites, as before; facts are always merged.
