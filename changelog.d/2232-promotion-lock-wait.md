- **The nightly skill-promotion stage/collect no longer skips a whole cycle silently on `promotion.lock` contention (#2232).** On the publisher, a 15-minute `collect --results-only` cron held the lock for ~17 s exactly when the 22:30 autosave sweep reached its promotion block. Stage and collect both tried the lock once, returned `ok: true, status: "locked"`, and the cycle processed nothing (cursor unchanged, no intake PR) with no failure signal anywhere. `ccc-skill-promotion.py run|collect` now accept `--lock-wait SEC` (0–600, default 0 = the previous single try). When a caller asks to wait and still loses the lock, the result is `ok: false, code: "lock_timeout"` (exit 2). The autosave sweep passes `--lock-wait` to both stage and collect (`CCC_SKILL_PROMOTION_LOCK_WAIT_SEC`, default 120; malformed or out-of-range values fall back to 120). A short overlap is absorbed, and a long one goes through the logged failure branch and lands in `last-collect.json` as `ok: false`. A new read-only doctor row, `skill-promotion last collect`, warns when `last-collect.json` is `ok: false` (with a lock-specific hint for `lock_timeout`) or shows the legacy `status: "locked"` skip. Callers that do not pass `--lock-wait` keep the benign `ok: true` locked result.
