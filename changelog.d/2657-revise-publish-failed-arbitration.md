- **A revise round whose result cannot be republished no longer strands the intake PR (#2657).** `_ARBITRATION_TERMINAL_REVISE` now includes `publish-failed` (the revised tree was refused on republish, e.g. `description_trigger_missing`) and `unchanged` (the reviser returned the same tree), so the arbitration sweep treats them as `revise_failed` like `failed`/`invalid`. Before, they fell through to "nothing to do" — `revise_stuck` only fires when no revise was dispatched — and fleet-skills#654 sat open for six days although its revise task had succeeded on the broker in two minutes. A new test reads every `a2a-revise-result` record literal in the publisher and fails if any written status is left unclassified.
