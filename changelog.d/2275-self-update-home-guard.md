- **self-update and `install-nunchi.sh` derive `HOME` when the launcher gives none (#2275).** A manual self-update started with `systemd-run` (no inherited environment) re-applied `scripts/install-nunchi.sh` on 2026-10-12 and the installer died under `set -u` at `CLAUDE_DIR="${CCC_CLAUDE_DIR:-$HOME/.claude}"` ("HOME: unbound variable"), so the tick ended `reapply-failed` with the crontab restored, the next attempt reported `activation-incomplete`, and the owner received two alarms for what the third retry then completed. `ccc-self-update.sh` now exports `HOME` from the shell's passwd/tilde fallback when it is unset (every installer it re-applies inherits it; a caller-set `HOME` is never overridden), and `install-nunchi.sh` carries the same guard for standalone runs. Tests: a HOME-less tick in `ccc-self-update.test.sh` re-applies a stub installer that records the `HOME` it saw; `install-nunchi.test.sh` runs `--status` with `HOME`/`CODEX_HOME` unset and asserts no unbound-variable death. Operators launching the script by hand should still pass `--property=Environment=HOME=…` (bridge-detached-self-restart skill); this change makes the omission harmless instead of alarming.
