- `scripts/a2a-termux-native-worker.sh`, `scripts/ccc-self-update.sh` (#2791 stage 2b): new `restart-worker --if-idle` (SIGTERM only the supervisor's `dist/worker.js` child; the supervise loop relaunches it, tunnel stays up) and `restart-supervisor --if-idle` (stop + respawn through the health check's `--self-heal` path). Idle keeps #2138's meaning on the worker's process tree — a descendant younger than `A2A_NATIVE_WORKER_BUSY_MAX_SECONDS` (default `CCC_SELF_UPDATE_BUSY_MAX_SECONDS` or 1800) is a running handler; older ones count as hung and do not block. Busy exits 10 and signals nothing; an unreadable tree fails closed (11); a restart is only reported ok after a new worker child, a fresh `registered with` log line, a settle window and health `sup_count=1 workers=1 tunnel=UP` (otherwise 30). There is no unconditional mode. Self-update still only warns about a stale `worker.js` (its restart belongs to the deploy side); a stale supervise loop of its own checkout is restarted only on nodes that opt in with `CCC_SELF_UPDATE_NATIVE_SUPERVISOR_RESTART=1` (default 0), and a busy worker defers to the next tick.
