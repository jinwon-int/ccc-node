- fleet-hande-watch no longer reports every empty answer as `UNREACHABLE` (#2673): only an ssh transport failure (exit 255) is `UNREACHABLE <node> channel=hande`; a probe that ran out of time is `UNVERIFIED … inspection=probe-timeout` and one that died without output is `UNVERIFIED … inspection=probe-error rc=<n>` (UNVERIFIED confirms over 3 runs instead of 2). The per-attempt limit goes from 10s to 20s and is set with `CCC_FLEET_HANDE_TIMEOUT` (1–30). On 2026-11-06 the T2 watcher (RTT ~340 ms) needed 7.5–12.2s for this probe against a healthy node, so gongmyoung flapped `UNREACHABLE` for 42–45 min four times in two days.
