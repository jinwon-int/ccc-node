- **Skill promotion can settle every unsettled intake lineage by A2A arbitration instead of waiting for an owner decision (#2435 S2, default OFF).** New publisher switch `skill-promotion.arbitration` / `CCC_SKILL_PROMOTION_ARBITRATION` (`off` | `dry-run` | `true`). A collect-drain sweep re-derives, from the ledger, every open intake PR whose latest state is `reject`, the revise round-limit handoff, a drop recommendation, a failed/invalid revise, a malformed review (after the S1 infrastructure retry) or a revise that went nowhere for 24 h, and dispatches one arbitration review round per lineage to a reviewer excluding the author and every node that reviewed or revised the lineage, with a metadata-only lineage-history appendix added to the worker procedure. The verdict is applied mechanically: `approve` follows the normal promote path; `revise` dispatches one final revise round beyond the round limit (any trusted substitute may take it); `reject`, an arbitration round without a verdict, an undeliverable final round, a lineage unsettled again after its arbitration, or no arbitration dispatched within 48 h closes the intake PR (`a2a-arbitration-closed`, lifecycle `archived:arbiter-closed` / `archived:arbiter-unavailable`, reopenable). The round-limit comment names the arbitration when the switch is `true`. `_revise_round_gate` and `_consume_malformed_verdict` were extracted to keep `_dispatch_intake_revise` / `_process_verdicts` under the complexity gate. New suite `scripts/ccc_skill_promotion_arbitration_test.py` (wired in `validate-harness.sh`); docs in `docs/skill-autosave.md`.
