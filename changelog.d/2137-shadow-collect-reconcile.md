- **gh-pr-flow:** `delegated_approval_reconcile.py` adds two read-only helpers for a delegated-approval window verdict (#2137, spec §10.2): `collect` copies every node's shadow ledger into one directory and names the nodes without a recorder, and `reconcile` lists the PRs GitHub closed in the window, classifies them with the recorder's rules, and reports the docs/test-only ones a person refused (closed unmerged, changes requested, `⛔ hold`, reverted within seven days) plus how many merges the ledgers never saw. Neither writes a ledger row or touches an approval.
