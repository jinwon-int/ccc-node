- **Span rollup: cost-ledger cross-check buckets turns by end time (#2684).** `spans-rollup.py` compared start-bucketed turn spans against ledger rows stamped at turn end (`ts`), so a turn crossing midnight counted on different days on the two sides — a one-turn drift that breached the < 5 % target on quiet days (live: yukson 5.88 %, gwakga 7.76 % on 2026-11-16, both from a single 23:58–00:02 turn). The cross-check now uses turns that *finished* in the KST day (`start_ts + duration_ms`, new `read_ledger_turns` reading one lookback day) and reports `turn_basis: "end"`; the day rollup itself (totals, tools, models, read stats) stays start-bucketed. Replayed on real spans from 5 nodes × 4 days: every day within target (yukson/gwakga 11-16 → 0.0 %). Two new tests in `scripts/spans_rollup_test.py`.
