- **Detached jobs (#2778): a job scheduled again under the same log is no longer swept as lost while its timer is armed.** `detached_jobs.py classify_one` asked about a scheduled job's `.timer` only when the log did not exist. `schedule` under the same unit and log leaves the previous run's output in that log, so once it had been quiet for 15 minutes the SessionStart sweep and `list` reported the job under "⚠ 소식 없음" (`lost`) although the timer had simply not fired — an invitation to investigate or re-run a healthy job. The `.timer` is now also asked about on the quiet-log path (through `systemctl --user` for a `scope=user` record), the same order the bridge-side probe uses since #2728. Unchanged: a record without `scheduled_at` never asks about a timer and is judged as before; a timer that is gone with no marker after the recorded offset is still `lost`; a fresh log is still running without any `systemctl` call; the non-systemd path. Completion notices and the job itself were never affected.
