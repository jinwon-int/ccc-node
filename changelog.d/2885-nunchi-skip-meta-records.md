- **nunchi no longer stores meta-records as facts (#2885).** Distillation sometimes stored the agent's own retrieval as a fact ("X는 제공된 근거에서 확인되지 않았다", "'기록 없음'으로 답변했다", "could not be verified in the provided context"); such rows describe what one session's context lacked and later surface in recall as if they were answers. `ingest()`, the write path of every feed, now drops a row when every sentence of it reports a negative finding that points at the retrieval context (or an answer of "no record"), with no clause joined before it and only a hedge after it; a row with a real finding next to the hedge is kept, as is a bare "not confirmed" with no context reference, and `constraint`/`preference`/`decision`/`procedure`/`correction` rows are never dropped. The summary line gains `skipped_meta_records=`. Rows already stored are not touched. On a read-only copy of one node's fact store it matches 23 of 1,905 open facts (1.2%); a hand check of 20 random matches found no row with a real finding. `NUNCHI_SKIP_META_RECORDS=0` stores them as before. `claude/hooks/nunchi/meta-records.test.sh` pins the behaviour on a synthetic fixture.
