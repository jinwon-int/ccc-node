- `mempalace-refresh.sh` now records the index consistency after every successful sweep (#2693): it runs the read-only `mempalace repair-status` (bounded by `CCC_NUNCHI_MEMPALACE_REPAIR_STATUS_TIMEOUT_SEC`, default 30 s) and writes `index_status: {state: ok|diverged|unknown, sqlite, hnsw}` into `mempalace-refresh.status.json`, classified by the installed `hooks/ccc_memory_probe.py` `parse_repair_status` that `ccc-memory-check.sh` already uses. A node's refresh status said `state=ok` for nine days while 80% of its HNSW index was missing (#2659). The #2336 flush-lag `OK` records `ok`; a missing parser, failing or hung `repair-status` or unparseable output records `unknown` and never fails the refresh; the report body is never stored. `ccc-memory-check.sh --json` projects it as `mempalace.refresh.index_status` and reports a recorded `diverged` as the existing `index-diverged` reason (so the #2692 doctor-sweep `MEMORY` column picks it up unchanged) when the live read is inconclusive — once, and never over a fresher live `ok`. Audience-scoped Piri lanes, which have no live per-scope read, count it as `refresh_index_diverged` and degrade with `index-diverged`.
