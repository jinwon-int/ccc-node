- **policy: conditional bundled approval for merge → pilot → fleet chains (#2617).** `gh-pr-flow` gains a "Conditional bundled approval" section and `CLAUDE.md.template` a pointer to it: one owner phrase (`<n> 묶음 승인`) can authorize a pre-declared chain — exact-head cross-account review + merge, then deploys built from the resulting `main` that carry an idle gate, backup and automatic rollback — with a machine-checkable pass condition before each later step. The bundle stops (and the agent asks again) on a moved head, a failed or unreadable condition, any `ROLLED_BACK`, an unlisted target, 24 h elapsed, or an owner `hold`/`중단`; a no-change `ABORT_BUSY` retries within the bundle. The template's Fresh Approval Required items, external-author PRs, broker/Gateway restarts and owner-observation-only gates are never bundled. It is a conversational owner approval, outside the a2a-nexus#2317 delegated-approval matrix, and does not lower its L3 set. Also states how a pasted-back option reads: exactly one option selects it; several exclusive options or a bare conditional sentence is not an approval. Motivation: family-messenger#635 needed three separate approval round trips (merge, pilot, fleet); CI completion → merge waits measured at median 19–26 min, p75 50–167 min.
