- **Fleet alert re-notify now repeats until answered, waits out the night, and stops on recovery (#2674).** An `action`/`critical` alert is re-notified up to 3 times — 15 min, 1 h and 4 h after its Matrix receipt (`⏰ 재통지 k/3 #id …`, `남은 재통지 N회`) — instead of once; `ok/hold <id>` stops the chain as before. A 2nd/3rd re-notify due 00:00–07:00 KST waits for 07:00 and the 3rd keeps its 3 h gap after the 2nd (the 1st is never deferred). Senders can now end a chain explicitly: an alert text line `@fleet-alert opens=<key>` / `@fleet-alert resolves=<key>` is stripped from what the rooms show, and a `resolves` key gives every open alert *of the same node* with that `opens` key a `resolved` receipt; a resolution-only alert is never re-notified. `fleet_watch_state.py` emits `fw:<node>:<channel>:<abnormalSince>` for NEW/STILL (opens) and RECOVERED (resolves); agent-cron alarms emit `ac:task|node|dead:<id digest>:<alertedAt>` and their "cleared" notice resolves it. Existing queues need no migration (the #2413 single `renotify` receipt counts as the 1st); `receipt_view` adds `renotify_count` and `resolved`.
